Fund selectors and manager selection teams rarely make allocation decisions on performance alone. Performance sits inside a wider due diligence framework used to assess whether a manager is skilled, their performance repeatable, and ultimately if they’re appropriate for a mandate. To make this assessment, the interpretation of performance is key.
